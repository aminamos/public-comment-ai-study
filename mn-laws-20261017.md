# Minnesota session laws slice — 2026-10-17

Source: Office of the Revisor of Statutes, `revisor.mn.gov/laws/2025/0/`
(2025 Regular Session, 39 chapters enumerated). The MN Secretary of State
site (sos.mn.gov) also hosts law PDFs (e.g. compiled election laws); the
Revisor is the comprehensive chapter-by-chapter source used here.

## Method

- Seed-42 random sample of 20 of 39 chapters; body-only text extracted from
  the Revisor HTML (site chrome and "new text begin/end" markup annotations
  removed mechanically; law text verbatim otherwise).
- Fit-to-remaining-cap selection ($49.75 left under laws $1,200 cap):
  16 chapters scored whole, one law = one row (`pangram-4` bulk).
  Chapters 20, 37, 38, 39 (60k–130k words each) deferred as over-cap.
- Spend: $31.75 (635 units).

## Result (n=16)

16/16 Human, 0 AI, 0 Mixed. Consistent with the federal slice
(255/256 Human) and the 18-ordinance slice (18/18 Human).
