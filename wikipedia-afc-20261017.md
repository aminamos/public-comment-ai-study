# Wikipedia Articles for Creation (2026-10-17)

Caveat: AI detection is probabilistic, not proof; a flag means the text statistically resembles AI output.

Frame: MediaWiki search "AFC submission" in the Draft namespace (11,066 hits; 6,496 titles collected). Random samples seed=42; wikitext mechanically stripped of markup (no rewriting), one draft = one row scored whole (Pangram bulk, pangram-4). Drafts under 50 words stored unscored — most AfC drafts are very short (first sample: 82/800 scorable).

Scored: 3,330 drafts (3,271 with a prediction; 59 items returned no prediction). Results: AI 620, Mixed 405, Human 2,246 — 31.3% flagged AI+Mixed, 19.0% AI alone, the highest flagged share of any track. Outcomes available in the Draft namespace are pending (3,286) and declined (44); accepted drafts leave the namespace, so no accepted-vs-rejected comparison is possible from this frame. Data in D1 `wikipedia_afc` and R2 `wikipedia-afc/`. Spend $477.90 / $500 cap.
